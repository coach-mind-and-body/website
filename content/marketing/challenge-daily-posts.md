## Day 2 — Flip it

🔎 DAY 2: FLIP IT!

Today's assignment is officially ON.

Grab TWO similar foods you actually eat or buy.

Two yogurts. Two breads. Two protein bars. Two cereals. Two creamers. Whatever makes sense for YOU.

Now... FLIP THEM OVER.

Compare:

🥣 Ingredients
🍬 Added sugar
📏 Serving size
💪 Protein
🌱 Fiber
🥖 Total carbs
🥑 Fat

Then come back here and tell us:

1️⃣ What did you compare?
2️⃣ What surprised you?
3️⃣ Which would you choose now — and WHY?

BONUS POINTS: 📸 Post a picture of both labels so we can look at them together.

And remember...

This isn't:
❌ “Which one makes me good?”
❌ “Which one am I allowed to eat?”

It's:
✅ “Now that I have more information, what choice do I want to make?”

THAT'S the skill we're building.

And if the Added Sugars line made you raise an eyebrow today... just wait. 😂 Tomorrow we're talking SUGAR. 👀
