**Business fit:** Mindset + habits + midlife metabolism education + coaching (not MD, not meal plans)  
**Date:** 2026-08-10

---

## 1. Why this feels ridiculous (truth)

Your GSC shows **~0 clicks** because:

1. **Almost no one searches your brand** yet (`mind body coach` ranks high but has tiny demand).
2. **You barely show for problem searches** (menopause belly, weight after 40, food noise, night cravings) — those are where the volume is.
3. **Your only real impression wedge** is product language: `feel great system` → Unicity page.
4. Mayo Clinic, Healthline, Cleveland Clinic, Midi, big media own head terms. **You win on mid-tail questions + a clear point of view.**

Search interest in menopause/perimenopause has risen for years (commercial + informational boom; menopause market ~$18B+). Women are online asking “what’s wrong with me?” and “why won’t this belly go away?” — not “R.E.C.L.A.I.M. coaching Utah.”

## 2. How women 40+ search (the language map)

They search **symptoms and frustrations**, then **causes**, then **solutions**.

### Stage A — “Something’s wrong” (huge volume, competitive)
| They type | Intent |
|-----------|--------|
| perimenopause symptoms | Am I in it? |
| menopause symptoms | Checklist / validation |
| hot flashes / night sweats | Classic |
| why am I so tired all the time | Often hormonal/sleep |
| brain fog women | Word-finding, focus |
| anxiety or perimenopause | You already have a post |
| irregular periods 40s | Diagnosis curiosity |

### Stage B — Weight & body (your money cluster)
| They type | Intent |
|-----------|--------|
| weight gain after 40 | Why is this happening |
| menopause weight gain | Hormone story |

Research context: midlife weight gain is common (~1–1.5 lb/year patterns often cited); fat redistributes to abdomen as estrogen drops — this is why “menopause belly” content dominates.

### Stage C — Food brain (your coaching superpower + rising trend)
| They type | Intent |
